# Choreography and source selection

These are generation and recovery notes, not a verbatim prompt transcript.
The original generated PNGs are preserved. The model is the user-approved
right-facing six-pose Attack 1: a small angular black-haired head, slim navy
coat, pale shirt, narrow legs, small skin hands and two short crimson daggers.
All other sheets preserve that identity and coarse pixel shading.

Rows in four-direction sources are down, left, right, up. Q/E each use a
single-facing sheet with six columns and two chronological rows. The approved
right Attack 1 and correction sheets have three columns and two rows.

contact cells were interchanged; the builder restores them to their intended
