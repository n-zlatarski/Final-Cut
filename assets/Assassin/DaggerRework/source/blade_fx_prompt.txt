Production VFX sprite atlas for a coarse 16-bit pixel RPG. Effects ONLY, no characters, weapons, scenery, text, labels or grid lines. EXACTLY SIX columns and THREE rows, eighteen separate equal square cells in an evenly spaced grid. Each row is ONE six-frame animation: 1 birth, 2 growth, 3 sharp contact peak, 4 extension, 5 broken fragments, 6 nearly faded tiny fragments. Transparent RGBA background, all blank space truly transparent. Every effect fully inside its cell, at least 20% blank margin on all sides. Each cell imagined at 64x64 native pixels; chunky integer pixel clusters, hard pixel edges, no antialiasing, no smooth gradients or bloom. Restrained thin trails only 2 to 4 native pixels thick. Most of each cell is empty. Consistent scale across phases, frame 6 visibly much smaller than frame 3. Right-facing / rightward motion. Crimson palette maroon #711e2e, red #cb2b41, coral #ff746c, tiny ivory #f1efeb accents. No orange fire, no purple, no filled white crescent, no circular aura, no big explosion. Narrow readable blade paths with sparse fragments, not giant sword effects.
